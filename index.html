About Avanish Singh Visen

Avanish grew up in a small town, in a family that farmed.

His grandfather and his father taught him two things he still carries: never give up on your dreams, and never give up your integrity. School was a pleasure because he loved to learn and explore, and that curiosity has stayed with him. He still says it plainly: most success starts with where you come from.

As a leader

Machines build products, but people build companies, he says. He coaches his managers to soften their intensity, so that employees, partners and clients never feel overwhelmed, because everyone is human before the role they play.

His rule at work is speed and quality. He decides quickly, and he undoes a wrong call just as quickly: Admitting failure is difficult but refusing to change bad decisions is dangerous. The one thing he says he has had to let go of as a leader is needing to be right, every time.

When recognition comes, he hands it to his people: Thank you, my dear team, for all your grit and grace under pressure.

As a person

God, Nature and Family are key driving forces, he says. Away from work he meditates, writes poetry and spends his time with his parents and family. He measures success quietly: I think success is the inner sense of satisfaction.

Life is all about relationships and making memories.

Two decades, one direction.
