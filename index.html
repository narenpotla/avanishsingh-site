About

Leading with speed. Building with quality.

Avanish Singh Visen is the Director and CEO of DCJ Group, home to APPL, Encraft and Enzocraft. Over two decades he has worked across automotive, IT, home appliances, polymer and fenestration, with Lenovo, Videocon and Mahindra.

He rose from SCM Head to CEO within the group, and built it on one rule: move fast, and never compromise on quality.
