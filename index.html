About

A surgeon shaped by purpose, not just training.

Dr. Sivaiah Potla grew up in rural Andhra Pradesh, studying under temple lights with determination. He secured merit seats for both MBBS and MS Orthopaedics at Guntur Medical College, then trained in Singapore, Germany, and the United States.

He returned home to bring world-class joint replacement care to Andhra Pradesh. Today, he leads Happy Hospitals as Founder and Chief Surgeon, serving patients from India and abroad with precision and compassion.
